Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take? Usually 2–4 days depending on complexity and revisions.
Can you help me select music and footage? Yes — music selection is central to the process, not an afterthought. We source tracks that match the property's character and your brand's tone.
Do you offer vertical videos for Instagram? Absolutely — Reels and TikTok formats are fully supported.
What's your pricing? Pricing is tailored to each project's scope and requirements. Contact us to discuss your vision and receive a custom quote.
How many revisions are included? Three rounds of revisions are included with every project. Additional changes beyond that are scoped and quoted individually.
Do you add subtitles or motion graphics? Yes — animated callouts, branded lower thirds, and logo animations are all available.
Is drone footage editing included? Yes — drone footage is integrated seamlessly and is often central to the narrative.
Can you handle urgent delivery? Yes, rush delivery options are available for time-sensitive listings.
