Questions
How long does a project take?

Typically 2–4 days, depending on length and complexity. Rush delivery is available for time-sensitive listings.

What formats do you deliver in?

All standard formats — 16:9 for YouTube and property portals, vertical 9:16 for Instagram Reels and TikTok, and square 1:1 when needed.

Can you help with music selection?

Yes. Music selection is central to the process. I source tracks that align with the property's character and the mood your brand commands.

How many revisions are included?

Three rounds of revisions are included with every project. Additional changes beyond that are scoped and quoted individually.

What is your pricing?

Pricing is tailored to each project's scope and requirements. Contact us to discuss your vision and receive a custom quote.

Do you work with drone footage?

Yes — drone footage is integrated seamlessly and is often central to the narrative, particularly for estate and development projects.

Do you add motion graphics or text overlays?

Yes. Animated callouts, branded lower thirds, and logo animations are all available and tailored to your visual identity.
